# zero-matrix-company

> Public project overview. The source repository is private.

An internal company-level source-of-truth repository for **Zero Matrix Technology**, used to keep company identity, brand standards, compliance documents, product ownership information and long-term operational records consistent and versioned.

## Scope

- Company identity and public-information standards
- Brand and visual-identity assets
- Legal and compliance documentation
- Product ownership and brand relationships
- Versioned operational records

The repository itself remains private because it contains internal company materials and source documents.
