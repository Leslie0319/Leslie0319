# Zero Matrix

> Public project overview. The implementation repositories are private.

**Zero Matrix** is a group of engineering projects covering a company website, structured financial-content standards, deterministic validation/rendering pipelines and local automation tooling.

## Repository family

- [`zero-matrix-company`](./zero-matrix-company.md) — company identity, brand standards, compliance and internal source-of-truth materials
- [`zero-matrix-web`](./zero-matrix-web.md) — official company website
- [`zero-matrix-finance-content`](./zero-matrix-finance-content.md) — versioned financial-content standards and validation rules
- [`zero-matrix-content-tools`](./zero-matrix-content-tools.md) — local content-production and validation tooling

## Engineering themes

- Structured data rather than free-form output as the source of truth
- Explicit validation and quality gates
- Versioned templates and reproducible outputs
- Traceability through manifests and hashing
- Clear human-verification boundaries around AI-assisted workflows

Only these public overview pages are exposed. Source repositories and internal documentation remain private.
